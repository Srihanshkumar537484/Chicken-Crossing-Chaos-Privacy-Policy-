Privacy Policy — Chicken Crossing: Chaos

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This privacy policy describes how Chicken Crossing: Chaos ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

Summary

Chicken Crossing: Chaos is a single-player game with no account and no login. The App shows ads (via Google AdMob) to support free play, and those ads are served under child-safe settings. Other than standard advertising data handled by Google AdMob, the App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob — a bottom banner ad, an occasional interstitial (full-screen) ad after a game ends, and an optional rewarded video ad if you choose "REVIVE (WATCH AD)" after a game over. Watching the rewarded ad is always optional; declining it does not block any part of the game.

This app is directed at, and is expected to be played by, children. Because of that, every ad request made by this App is configured before it is sent, so that:

  • Ads are served only from Google Play-certified, child-safe ad networks
  • Ad content is capped to Google's "G" (general audiences) content rating
  • The request is tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, which disables personalised/interest-based advertising and ad tracking for this App
  • No behavioural advertising profile is built from App usage

To serve ads at all, Google AdMob's SDK may process technical data such as a temporary advertising identifier, coarse device/network information, and ad interaction events (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped), consistent with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (including COPPA). This processing is performed by Google as the App's ad service provider, not by us directly, and is limited by the child-safe settings above. For details on how Google AdMob itself handles this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob-specific information.

What data we collect ourselves

None. Outside of the AdMob advertising data described above, the App does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or any other device content
  • Any data to any server we operate (we do not operate any server — the App has no backend of its own)

What is stored, and where

The App saves your game progress (coins, best score, unlocked levels, unlocked skins, achievements, settings, etc.) using Android's standard local SharedPreferences storage, entirely on your own device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device and is never sent to us
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually if you clear the App's storage/data from Android Settings, or if you use the in-App "Reset Progress" option in Settings

Permissions used

The App requests the following:

  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback (a short buzz) when your chicken is hit by traffic, if enabled in Settings. Grants no access to personal data.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required by the Google AdMob SDK to request and display ads. The game itself has no online features and does not use the internet for anything else (no login, no cloud save, no analytics of our own).

Children's privacy

This App is expected to be played by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children ourselves. Ads shown in the App are configured with Google's child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings as described in the Advertising section above, and Google Play Families Policy requirements should be reviewed and completed in Play Console's "Target audience and content" section before publishing.

Changes to this policy

If the App's data or advertising practices change further, this policy will be updated first, and the new version will be reflected in the App's Play Store listing before that update is released.
